Reality transitions

Every place you visit has an onto address — a coordinate not just in space but across several axes of reality. Ordinary travel (solid blue links) moves you within one reality. A reality transition shifts you sideways or deeper into a different version of it, spawning a fresh copy of the map to explore.

Each axis below has a forward command and a matching back to undo it. Colours match the transition links on the map, and costs are what each hop spends against your budget when a game is in force.

Playing the game

When a game is in force you start with a finite budget that every move spends against. A move you cannot afford is refused and costs nothing. Your goal is a quest of round trips: reach each target coordinate in order and return home after each — the last return home wins. Returning home is always allowed even if it overspends. Your score is a 1–3 star rating for how close to par (the optimal cost) you played.
